AgentCard — Deploy

OWNER-ONLY · BNB CHAIN

Owner-only page. Deploy the AgentCard NFT contract on BNB Chain, open mint when ready, and withdraw the mint fees. Free Mint eligibility is read on-chain from the Binance Agent ID registry — no manual verification lists. Nothing here deploys or moves funds without your wallet confirmation.

The collection rules are fixed in the contract and cannot be changed:

  • Supply: 2,222 — 1,888 Free Mint + 334 Paid Mint, two independent pools. Neither pool blocks the other.
  • No phases: one mint switch opens Free and Paid at the same moment. Paid is mintable from the first second mint opens.
  • Free Mint: 0 BNB, max 2 per wallet, Binance Agent ID holders only — the contract checks the registry live at mint time. Always a Standard Issue Common card, no rarity roll.
  • Paid Mint: 0.0012 BNB, max 5 per wallet, open to every wallet — Agent ID holders included (they may mint both Free and Paid). Rarity is rolled inside the mint transaction: Common 50% · Rare 32% · Legendary 18%.
  • No pre-numbering: token IDs follow mint order from one counter. Each card's data file is chosen by its type: free.json · paid-common.json · paid-rare.json · paid-legendary.json.
  • Engraving: a card's owner can engrave a custom name (1–16 chars) for 0.0005 BNB per engraving, stored on-chain.
  • Fees stay in the contract until the owner withdraws them.
  • Mint starts CLOSED. Open it in step 03 when you are ready — opening it opens Free and Paid together.
